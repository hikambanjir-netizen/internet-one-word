One word for the whole internet. Not a place, not an app, not a mood — the whole wild thing, and the word it earns is the one you'd use for something unowned, untamed, and liable to bite.

> feral

$10 escrowed, top three share the reward (1st $5). No entry fee, three blind rounds. The judge reads entries two at a time and picks the funnier, better-fitting word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints exactly the one word and nothing else.
